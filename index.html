Island & Anikku+

Personal projects, used by the developer and a few friends. They are not offered to the public.

Island

A desktop shell for Linux (Hyprland). Its notepad widget can show your Google Tasks, and its anime player keeps your progress in sync with Anikku+.

Anikku+

A personal build of the Android anime app Anikku. It syncs your progress through a private file in your own Google Drive.
