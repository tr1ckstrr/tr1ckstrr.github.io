Privacy policy

Island (a desktop shell) and Anikku+ (an Android app) are personal projects, used only by the developer and a few friends.

What the apps access

  • Google Tasks (tasks): the notepad widget shows and edits your own task lists.
  • Google Drive app data (drive.appdata): a private sync file that keeps your anime progress the same on your phone and PC. The apps can't see any other files in your Drive.

What happens with the data

  • It stays on your own devices and in your own Google account.
  • Nothing is collected, sent to the developer, sold or shared with anyone.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on your devices. Disconnecting in the app removes them, and you can revoke access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
